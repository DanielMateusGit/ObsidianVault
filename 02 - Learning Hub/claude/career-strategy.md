## Figure Professionali Target

> **Aggiornato 2026-04-23 con cluster taxonomy data-driven.** Ogni "figura" si scompone in 1+ cluster specifici con stack/salary/timing diversi.

### 1. AI Engineer / AI-Native Engineer (TARGET PRIMARIO)
- **Cluster mappati:** AI #2 (GenAI/LLMOps) + AI #3 (Full-Stack AI-First) + SE #1 (AI-Native Backend)
- **Salary:** £85-200k UK (range ampio per cluster), €90-130k EU, €110-160k US
- **Tuo vantaggio competitivo nel 2026-2027**
- Combina software engineering solido + AI skills pratiche
- Ruolo in forte domanda: aziende cercano chi sa COSTRUIRE con AI, non solo usarla
- Il percorso copre: LLM integration, RAG, Agentic workflows, Evals, Guardrails, Multi-Agent, MCP, LangGraph

### 2. Senior Software Engineer (probabilita 95%)
- **Cluster mappati:** SE #5 (Modern Full-Stack) + SE #2 (Fintech Low-Latency)
- **Salary:** £52-130k UK (Modern Full-Stack è bridge accessibile, Low-Latency è premium)
- Full-stack delivery + mentorship
- Fallback sicuro: le skill Senior sono la BASE su cui costruisci AI Engineer

### 3. Solutions Architect (probabilita 75-80%)
- **Cluster mappati:** AI #1 (AI Tech Lead Enterprise)
- **Salary:** €85-120k EU, €110-150k US
- Richiede AZ-305 + soft skills + leadership track
- Long-game (18+ mesi)

### 4. Staff Engineer (probabilita 60-70%)
- **Cluster mappati:** SE #3 (Infrastructure/SRE) come bridge
- **Salary:** €100-140k EU, €120-160k US
- Spesso richiede 6-8 anni (potresti entrare Senior e promuovere)

### 5. AI-Augmented SWE / Growth Engineer (NUOVO bridge ⭐ — categoria emergente 2026)
- **Cluster mappato:** AI #4 (AI-Augmented SWE)
- **Salary:** IT €40-65k / UK £70-100k
- **Bridge accessibile più rapido per Dan** (3-6 mesi)
- Profilo: SWE backend che usa AI per amplificare business outcomes (sales, marketing, GTM)
- Esempi: Jet HR, Homey

### 6. Founding / Product Engineer (categoria emergente premium UK)
- **Cluster mappati:** FE #4 (Founding FE) + FE #5 (AI-Native FE)
- **Salary:** UK £90-130k+
- Profilo: high-craft engineer in scale-up Series A/B (Edra, Jumpstart pattern)
- "Anti-ticket-queue" mindset, ownership totale, library/SDK building

## Direzioni Post-Percorso

### 1. Staff/Principal Engineer
- Distributed Systems deep dive, Platform Engineering, Performance Engineering
- Libri: "Staff Engineer" (Larson), "The Staff Engineer's Path" (Reilly)

### 2. Engineering Manager
- People management, Team Topologies, Product thinking
- Libri: "The Manager's Path" (Fournier), "An Elegant Puzzle" (Larson)

### 3. AI/ML Deep Specialization (NATURALE EVOLUZIONE da AI Engineer)
- Fine-tuning, RAG avanzato, Multi-agent orchestration, MLOps
- Libri: "Designing ML Systems" (Huyen), "AI Engineering" (Huyen 2025)
- Nota: il percorso AI Engineer ti da gia il 60% delle basi per questa direzione

### 4. Cloud/Platform Specialization
- Multi-cloud, Advanced K8s, SRE, FinOps
- Cert: CKS, AWS SA Professional

### 5. Entrepreneurship
- Product Development, Marketing, Sales
- Libri: "The Lean Startup" (Ries), "The Mom Test" (Fitzpatrick)
